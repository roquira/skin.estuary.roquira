4.1.0-roquira.1
- First release, based on Estuary 4.1.0 from Kodi 22.
- Anime home category, with anime kept out of Movies and TV Shows.
- Countries row and country flags for movies.
- Reorderable home widgets and more TV show widgets.
- Option to hide episode ratings.
- Library update and clean items in the power menu, with the roquira CoreELEC build.
- Defaults to a shorter main menu and weather in the top bar, with episode ratings and the Recently Added Episodes widgets hidden.
